Q11 ML/DL Models Count

Validation:

- Required when Machine Learning or Deep Learning or Predictive Analytics is selected in Q10
- Must be numeric
- Must be a whole number
- Must be greater than or equal to 1

Message:

- ML/DL Model Count is required when Machine Learning or Deep Learning is selected.
- Enter a number greater than or equal to 1.

Note:

- If ML or DL is selected, this gives us a way to understand how many traditional ML/DL models are being used by the solution.



Q12 LLM Count

Validation:

- Required when Q10 contains LLM, Generative AI, or Agentic AI
- Must be numeric
- Must be a whole number
- Must be greater than or equal to 0

Message:

- LLM Count is required when Agentic AI, Generative AI, or LLM is selected.
- Enter a whole number greater than or equal to 0.


Note:

- This helps us understand the scale of LLM usage within the solution and supports AI inventory reporting
